Getting Here

Watamu is a stunning coastal gem on Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline, easy to reach from major airports.

Malindi Airport (MYD)

Closest airport — approx. 20 min drive. Daily flights from Nairobi (Wilson & JKIA).

Mombasa Airport (MBA)

International flights available. Approx. 1.5 – 2 hr scenic coastal drive to Watamu.

Airport Transfer

We can arrange a private transfer for you. Just share your flight details when booking.

Local Area

Watamu town is 5 min away. The famous Watamu Marine National Park is 10 min by boat.
